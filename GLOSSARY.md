# Refunds

Terms for a learner requesting money back for a course purchase and for the return of that money.

## Language

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to receive a refund. Its status is **Open** until it is **Approved** or **Declined**; submitting it does not mean money has been returned.

**Open**:
The status of a refund request that has not yet been approved or declined. In learner-facing copy, describe it as "under review."

**Approved**:
The decided status of a refund request that may proceed to refund processing. Approval alone does not mean the refund has been issued.

**Declined**:
The decided status of a refund request that will not proceed to refund processing.

**Refund**:
The return of the full amount paid for a purchase after a refund request is approved. Approval alone does not mean the refund has been issued.

**Refund Processing**:
The period after approval while the refund is being handled but has not yet been confirmed as issued. This is distinct from a refund request being under review.

**Refund Issued**:
A refund confirmed as sent to the original payment method. The credit may still take time to appear in the learner's account.

**Amount Paid**:
The full amount the learner paid for the purchase; this is the amount used for a full refund.
